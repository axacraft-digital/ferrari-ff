01 / The machine

Four seats.
Four driven wheels.
One glorious V12.

FF means “Ferrari Four”—four seats and four-wheel drive. Beneath the shooting-brake body sits a 6,262 cc naturally aspirated V12, a seven-speed dual-clutch gearbox and Ferrari's original 4RM all-wheel-drive concept.

This car was never treated as a disposable exotic. It was approached as a keeper: preserve the drama, understand the weak points, and engineer out as many compromises as practical.

02 / The build

Rebuilt beneath.
Refined throughout.

Driveline

The hard work, done.

The Getrag 7DCL750 dual-clutch transmission and the PTU were fully rebuilt. The DCT received a deep kit of bearings, sensors, seals and gaskets, while the PTU history includes a seal recall corrected by Hollywood Mechanic and later pressure-sensor work.

Clutch & response

Dodson, calibrated by Ferrari.

A serviceable Dodson street-performance clutch retains the standard plate count, adds grip, and was successfully calibrated by a Ferrari dealer. According to technician Micah Coley, this was the first Ferrari application of this particular Dodson street setup.

Heat management

Cooler by design.

A custom full-aluminum radiator with a real drain plug, replacement oil/water pump, thermostat and hoses, plus a discreet ducted fan system that can keep moving air after shutdown. The battery moved to the trunk, away from the engine bay's heat soak.

Engine & sound

V12 health, V12 voice.

Top-engine gaskets and seals, twelve NGK spark plugs, professionally cleaned injectors, intake-valve carbon cleaning and a replacement oil/water pump. The soundtrack comes through an installed FI Exhaust system and catalyst-free downpipes—and the original stock exhaust stays with the car.

Made to be driven.

03 / The chronology

Four years of thoughtful evolution.

2023

The mechanical—and visual—foundation

At 28,684 miles, Ferrari of Salt Lake City documented returning gearbox faults P0846 and P1907 and concluded that an internal failure required a rebuild. The subsequent work included full Getrag 7DCL750 and PTU rebuilds, steel-brake conversion, fluids, filters, belt and an owner-supplied exhaust swap, all documented by a paid invoice. The entire body also received flat-finish protective PPF, creating the satin appearance seen today.

Confirmed
2024

The comprehensive second act

Top-engine sealing, injectors, plugs, aluminum radiator, oil/water pump, PTU accumulator and pressure sensors, Dodson clutch, exhaust work, battery relocation and HVAC work were invoiced; the major mechanical modifications were later substantially corroborated during the physical walkthrough.

Corroborated
2025

Calibration and refinement

Ferrari dealer clutch calibration, four polyurethane gearbox bushings and the auxiliary engine-bay cooling system. A detailed owner/technician walkthrough then confirmed the installed configuration and captured the engineering rationale.

Confirmed
2026

Rear-axle renewal and invoice consolidation

Two replacement rear axles, part number 744019, were added to Invoice 432 and then explicitly confirmed installed by Micah Coley. A newly received consolidated revision adds Red Line 10W-60, a Ferrari oil filter and engine-oil-change labor. The heater-control-valve, A/C and oil-service lines still lack separate completion evidence.

04 / Good stories

The details that make this one different.

01

The auxiliary fans can keep cooling the engine bay after the ignition is off.

Temperature control and low-voltage protection help avoid both heat soak and a stranded battery.
02

The battery lives in the trunk—but under-hood positive and negative connections remain.

The relocation improves access and moves the battery away from the hottest part of the car.
03

The radiator finally has something the factory unit did not: a proper drain plug.

A small feature that makes regular coolant service substantially more practical.
04

Four polyurethane gearbox bushings sit front and rear, shielded from exhaust heat.

They sharpen drivetrain response while addressing age and heat degradation in the factory mounts.
05

The Dodson clutch is intentionally a street setup—not the extreme high-plate-count version.

More grip and serviceable seals, while preserving civilized Comfort-mode behavior.
06

The satin color is created by protection, not paint.

Flat-finish PPF covers the entire car, giving the body its distinctive satin character while shielding the finish underneath.

Service addendum

The small print.

A line-item view of the less visible work behind the larger story. This list is limited to service documented as completed or subsequently identified on the vehicle; estimates, recommendations and unresolved invoice-only items are omitted. Costs are intentionally not published.

DCT, PTU & clutch

  • Getrag 7DCL750 removed, fully rebuilt and reinstalled.
  • Main-shaft bearings, axle seals, internal seals, paper and valve-body gaskets, solenoid plugs and O-rings renewed.
  • Speed, clutch-temperature, clutch-pressure and system/e-diff pressure sensors renewed during the transmission work.
  • PTU removed and rebuilt with its gasket, seal, hose and fluid kit, additional flush fluid and a replacement bushing.
  • First PTU seal batch subsequently replaced under the supplier recall described by the technician.
  • PTU accumulator, two clutch-pressure sensors and the main system-pressure sensor replaced.
  • Dodson street-performance clutch installed with standard plate count and serviceable seals and plates.
  • Gearbox calibrated by a Ferrari dealer for the Dodson clutch.
  • Four polyurethane gearbox bushings installed front and rear, with heat shielding near the exhaust.

Engine, fuel & cooling

  • Valve-cover, manifold and timing-solenoid sealing work completed across the top of the engine.
  • Twelve NGK spark plugs installed.
  • Fuel injectors removed, professionally cleaned and balanced, resealed and reinstalled.
  • Intake-valve carbon cleaning completed.
  • Combined Ferrari oil/water pump replaced with thermostat and hoses.
  • Custom full-aluminum radiator installed with a proper drain plug.
  • Bosch coolant-temperature sensor at the lower radiator replaced.
  • Ducted auxiliary engine-bay fans installed with temperature control, after-run cooling and low-voltage protection.
  • Engine oil and filter, air filters and serpentine belt serviced during the 2023 rebuild program.

Brakes, axles & chassis

  • Carbon-ceramic brake system converted to steel GiroDisc rotors and pads.
  • Motul RBF 660 brake fluid documented with the brake conversion.
  • Ferrari Recall 80 completed: NQS software update, brake-fluid reservoir cap and owner-manual leaflet.
  • Wheel spacers and matching wheel bolts installed.
  • Both rear axles replaced in 2026 with part number 744019.
  • New Michelin Pilot Sport 4S Max Performance Summer tires installed.

Exhaust, electrical & exterior

  • FI Exhaust system and catalyst-free downpipes installed.
  • Original factory exhaust retained and included with the car.
  • Battery relocated to the trunk using custom brackets and cabling.
  • Under-hood positive and negative connection points retained after the relocation.
  • Entire body covered in flat-finish protective PPF, creating the satin appearance.
